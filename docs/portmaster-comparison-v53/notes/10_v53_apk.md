## Additional release changes
- `scripts/patchnotes.gd:10–25` contains V5.3 new/revised features. It states item-lock/discard behavior; chest/crate interaction, rift move-linking, run summary, gacha odds, thicker touch scroll bars, Kecleon shop fixes, reward sources, Scald burn chance, desktop window controls, Flamethrower tuning, fewer particles/effects for lag, ban-slot cap/cost tuning, and pause-screen redesign.
- Of these, the 5.3 note directly relevant to PortMaster is the reported reduction in particles/effects to reduce lag (`patchnotes.gd:21`). Treat as a benchmark/tuning candidate; the note does not identify the implementation files or quantify device-level gain.
- Other relevant stability/UI ideas include thicker touch scrollbars (`patchnotes.gd:16`) and fixes to interaction flow, but they are not among stages 1–5.
